# Changelog

All notable PSE: Modded Survivors changes.

## [0.1.0]

### Added

- Gunners: Executioner, Reclaimer (Hammer AR, Prybar AR, Chisel Shotgun), REX, Zerimp, Hunter, Technician's wrench, Captain, and Wisp, Greater Wisp, Archaic Wisp, Nautilus and Scavenger from Lose Humanity get a magazine, the active reload and mouse aim.
- Melee: Revenant, Nemesis Mercenary, Nemesis Commando, Technician, Gigachad, Jam, Gin, Fishmonger, MULE, and Lemurian, Elder Lemurian, Golem, Parent, Macrobic Predator and Tardigrade from Lose Humanity get stamina, the finisher and Rage, the series, the lunge and the launcher.
- The melee survivors' finisher shows a shared blast in the survivor's color, and their Rage gives +50% damage, 15% lifesteal and +20% attack speed.
- A charge window for MULE and Captain: release inside it for a full-charge shot and Steady Hand.
- Only the survivors whose mods are installed are affected.

# Changelog

All notable PSE: Modded Survivors changes.

## [0.1.0]

### Added

- Gunners: Executioner, Reclaimer (Hammer AR, Prybar AR, Chisel Shotgun), REX, Zerimp, Hunter, Technician's wrench, Captain, Wisp, Greater Wisp, Archaic Wisp, Nautilus and Scavenger from Lose Humanity, and Admiral, H.3.L.P.3.R. and Reclaimer from Worlds Incursion get a magazine, the active reload and mouse aim.
- Melee: Revenant, Nemesis Mercenary, Nemesis Commando, Technician, Gigachad, Jam, Gin, Fishmonger, MULE, Lemurian, Elder Lemurian, Golem, Parent, Macrobic Predator and Tardigrade from Lose Humanity, and Warrior from Worlds Incursion get stamina, the finisher and Rage, the series, the lunge and the launcher.
- The melee survivors' finisher shows a shared blast in the survivor's color, and their Rage gives +50% damage, 15% lifesteal and +20% attack speed.
- A charge window for MULE and Captain: release inside it for a full-charge shot and Steady Hand.
- Worlds Incursion's new primaries for vanilla survivors: HAN-D's ASSUALT is a gunner; Bandit's Scatter, Enforcer's Trenchboom and Engineer's Blastgun keep their own stock and get mouse aim.
- Only the survivors whose mods are installed are affected.
